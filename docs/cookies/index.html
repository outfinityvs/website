Cookies

This page describes the cookies and similar technologies used on the Outfinity website and the controls available to visitors.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors interact with the site. This page explains which technologies Outfinity uses and how you can control them.

Functional cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly. They enable core features such as navigation, page rendering, and access to secure areas. The website cannot function properly without these cookies. They do not store any personally identifiable information.

Analytics cookies

Outfinity may use analytics cookies to collect anonymized information about how visitors use the website. This includes pages visited, time spent on each page, referring website, and basic device information. This data helps us improve the website and understand which content is most relevant to our visitors. Analytics data is aggregated and not used to identify individual users.

Third-party services

The Outfinity contact form is hosted by Google Forms, which may set its own cookies as described in Google's privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Google's cookie practices for more information about how they handle data collected through embedded forms.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, and to set preferences for specific websites. Please note that blocking functional cookies may affect the performance and usability of the website. Analytics cookies can be opted out of through your browser settings or through the analytics provider's opt-out tools.

Updates to this page

This page is updated when the technologies used on the website change. Review it periodically for the most current information about how Outfinity supports browsing transparency.
