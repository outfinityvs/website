Outfinity Venture Studio [www.outfinity.ch]

For Creators and Operators

This page is for people who build. If you are a researcher with a prototype that could become a product, an engineer who can lead a development team, an operator who has taken a company from zero to revenue, or a domain expert who knows a regulated market deeply enough to spot the opportunity everyone else misses, venture-specific participation is how you join an Outfinity venture as a founder, a key contributor, or a commercial lead. You are not writing a cheque. You are bringing capability, time, judgement, and the kind of execution that turns a validated thesis into a real company.

When an opportunity reaches the point where it is ready for company formation, Outfinity works with the people who will carry it forward to define founder roles, equity structures, vesting schedules, and the operational plan that turns a venture thesis into a functioning business. The studio handles the legal and IP preparation so the company starts with clean ownership and a usable asset base. The people who have been contributing through the Explorer Circle, through validation sprints, or through paid project work are the natural candidates for these roles. New people with the right profile can also join at this stage if they bring something the venture genuinely needs. The conversation is practical: what does this venture need to succeed, who can provide it, and how should the ownership reflect that contribution.

For investors who prefer to participate in a specific venture rather than the platform as a whole, the same structures apply, NewCo equity, SPVs, convertible instruments, chosen to match the venture's maturity and the investor's profile. But the heart of this page is about the people who do the work. Outfinity exists to make their path into venture formation clearer, fairer, and more professionally structured than it would be if they had to figure it all out alone.
