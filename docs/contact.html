Reasons to Get in Touch

Investors and family offices reach out when they want a second opinion on an AI startup, a venture exploration memo before committing capital, or a technical due diligence review of a target company. The conversation begins with the specific opportunity, not with a request for investment.

Enterprises and corporate innovation teams contact Outfinity when they need a validation sprint for a concrete AI problem, a prototype to test feasibility before committing a larger budget, or an honest technical assessment of a proposed solution. The engagement stays commercial and the IP stays clean until both sides agree otherwise.

AI labs and research groups get in touch when they have technical assets, prototypes, or applied research that could become part of a venture path. Outfinity helps assess where that work fits: shared infrastructure, venture validation, or a company formation track. Founder-operators and domain experts contact us when they can lead a venture, validate a market, or bring the commercial and organisational capability that turns a thesis into a company.

Inquiry Routes

To help Outfinity route your inquiry effectively, please use the contact form and indicate which route best describes your situation:

Investor / Family Office

For private venture intelligence, technical due diligence on AI investments, validation sprints, or retainer discussions. Include your investment focus and technology interests.

Enterprise

For corporate innovation teams, family businesses, or technical firms that want to commission a validation sprint, prototype, or AI opportunity mapping.

AI Lab / Research Partner

For research groups with technical assets, prototypes, or applied research that could become part of a venture path.

Founder-Operator

For individuals who can lead a venture, validate a market, or bring domain expertise to company formation.

Technical Due Diligence Request

For investors needing an independent technical evaluation of a target company or project before committing capital.

Explorer Circle (Standard)

For researchers, builders, and experts seeking to join the working network through trusted referral or demonstrated contribution.

For AI Labs and Researchers

Reach out if you have technical assets, prototypes, infrastructure, methods, or applied research that could become part of a serious venture path.

Outfinity can help assess where that work fits: shared infrastructure, venture validation, a commercialization program, or a company-formation track.

For Founder-Operators and Experts

Reach out if you can lead a venture, validate a market, shape product direction, bring domain expertise, or help build the commercial and organizational side of a company.

Outfinity is especially interested in people who can translate a strong thesis into coordinated execution.

For Enterprises, Recruiters, and Investors

Strategic enterprises can bring workflows, pilot contexts, and market pressure. Recruitment partners can help identify rare talent. Investors can support the platform or a specific venture path with capital and strategic leverage.

Each of these profiles strengthens a different part of the Outfinity model.

What Makes a Strong Inquiry

A strong request explains who you are, what you bring, which opportunity or venture direction you care about, and what kind of collaboration or conversation you want to explore.

This helps Outfinity review the request quickly and move the conversation toward the most useful next step.

What Happens Next

After review, Outfinity may schedule a call, request more context, share a more focused path into the Explorer Circle, open a technical or investor discussion, or route the conversation toward a specific venture direction.

The process is designed to keep the first interaction short, relevant, and immediately useful for both sides.
