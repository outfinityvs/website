Outfinity Explorer Circle

The Explorer Circle is a selective access layer for people and organizations that may become relevant to Outfinity's venture pipeline.

Studio ModelHow Ventures Are BuiltExplorer Circle

What the Explorer Circle is

The Explorer Circle is not a public community and it is not a symbolic membership program. It is a curated layer through which selected investors, founder-operators, AI Labs, researchers, experts, strategic enterprises, and talent partners can receive access to parts of the Outfinity venture pipeline when that access is useful and justified.

The circle exists because venture formation often requires more discretion and context than a public website can support. Some conversations require deeper technical material, more candid discussion of market assumptions, or early visibility into venture directions that are not yet ready for broad exposure.

What access can look like

Depending on relevance and timing, Explorer Circle participants may receive selected venture updates, invitations to focused discussions, requests for technical or commercial feedback, and access to deeper documentation for specific projects. Access is always selective and contextual. It is shaped by usefulness, confidentiality, and the maturity of the opportunity.

That means not every participant sees the same material and not every conversation leads to the same kind of involvement. The Explorer Circle is a qualification and alignment layer, not a promise of equal visibility into every part of the studio.

What it does not grant

Explorer Circle status does not create employment, advisory rights, investment rights, exclusivity, or ownership over ideas, ventures, code, or research. It also does not guarantee access to all internal documentation or all future opportunities. Those questions belong to separate written agreements and project-specific decisions.

This distinction is important because the circle is designed to enable serious early conversations without creating false expectations. The goal is to improve venture formation, not to blur the boundary between access and entitlement.

How contributions and intellectual property are handled

Background intellectual property remains with its original owner unless it is separately licensed or transferred in writing. Open-source contributions to designated repositories are governed by the applicable repository license. Paid work, when it exists, is handled through explicit project agreements rather than by implication.

That legal clarity is not a bureaucratic add-on. It is part of the studio model. The earlier the collaboration becomes serious, the more important it is that rights, scope, and ownership are stated with enough precision to support a real venture later.
