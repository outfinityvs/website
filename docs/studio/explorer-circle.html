Outfinity Venture Studio [www.outfinity.ch]

Network

Most serious venture opportunities surface before a company exists, before a pitch deck is written, before the founding team is fully assembled. At that stage the most useful thing anyone can do is connect the right people around the right thesis and give them enough context to act. The Explorer Circle is how Outfinity does that. It brings researchers, engineers, entrepreneurs, domain experts, and investors into the conversation early enough that their contribution can genuinely change the shape of what gets built. Membership is lean and grows through people who have already proven they can deliver. That keeps introductions useful and trust high from the first conversation.

Some participants contribute to open-source infrastructure that Outfinity keeps reusable across ventures. Those contributions follow the license of the relevant repository and strengthen the shared technical base that future companies start from. Others work on paid, project-specific engagements where scope, deliverables, and IP terms are defined clearly from the beginning, so compensation and future participation rights are never ambiguous. The paid tier of the Explorer Circle, structured as a Private Venture Intelligence Retainer, gives investors and strategic partners structured access to private research, selective documentation, and early visibility into formation candidates. Experts, researchers, and entrepreneurs who collaborate closely with Outfinity may receive paid project engagements and visibility into venture outcomes where their contribution is directly relevant. Everyone knows what they are building, what they own, and how they can participate further when the venture matures.
