About These Ventures

These are internal ventures associated with Outfinity's research projects. We publish them to attract researchers, investors, and collaborators whose interests or investment theses are complementary to the directions we are exploring. Each venture below represents a concrete area where applied research, technical infrastructure, and market opportunity intersect at different levels of maturity.

Not all ventures in the Outfinity pipeline are public. Some remain in early exploration or under confidentiality constraints and are shared only through direct engagement, private research memos, or structured conversations with qualified partners and investors.

Maturity Levels

Each venture direction is classified according to its current stage in the formation pipeline. This helps investors, partners, and collaborators understand what level of engagement is appropriate.

Research thesis. The technical foundation and market hypothesis are being explored and documented. Evidence is being gathered but the direction has not yet reached formal validation. Engagement: research collaboration, co-investigation, or a Venture Exploration Memo.

Validation candidate. The thesis is defined and active validation work is underway: technical research, market interviews, architecture design. Engagement: join the validation sprint, provide domain expertise, or fund targeted research.

Prototype candidate. A controlled prototype exists or is being built to demonstrate the core technical approach. Engagement: contribute to prototype development, test the prototype in a real-world context, or commission a Prototype & Feasibility Package.

Company formation candidate. The opportunity has sufficient evidence for incorporation. NewCo structure, team, IP assignment, and financing are being prepared. Engagement: founder-operator roles, venture-specific investment, or strategic partnership.
