Investment Teaser

The opportunity

Large Language Models have made AI fluent. They have not made it verifiable. They answer whether or not they understood the question, their outputs are hard to reproduce, audit or certify, and every request runs on specialised hardware, usually in the cloud. This keeps AI away from the places where errors are expensive: the execution of business operations, official records, regulated decisions and machines acting in the physical world. What is missing is a layer of understanding that can be checked, and that is cheap enough to sit in front of every action. Better still, that layer can be deterministic, fast and efficient enough to run directly on CPUs without or with a small LLM.

The invitation

The Proposed Venture and its initial technology

We invite you to join the Founders Consortium to establish the Axiologic AI Lab in Switzerland, dedicated to developing the VSS technology. VSS (VSA Semantic Space) is a new approach to machine understanding discovered by Sînică Alboaie, PhD, at Axiologic Research (Iași, Romania). VSS turns information into an explicit structure that people and systems can read and check. Three core properties set VSS apart from dominant approaches.

Scientific evidence

Initial validation

The approach has passed a first level of scientific validation, on controlled benchmarks and on a public real-world dataset, against a neural network given the same inputs. On the axes that matter most it is clearly ahead: it handles words never seen in training at 100% accuracy, against 25% for the neural baseline; it composes structures one level deeper than anything seen in training at close to 100%, against 39%; and on real questions over databases it identifies what is asked at 82%, against 54%, and stays ahead at every training size tried. A complete model takes only a few megabytes and runs very fast on CPUs. VSS and its associated symbolic reasoning create deterministic systems.

The initial results are published at zenodo.org/records/23476296 and we also published a free book on scriptahub.com that describes the high level approach. While additional results and insights exist, this should be sufficient for public disclosure and initial scientific validation. The public documents indicate that what remains is mainly scaling and engineering, step by step, rather than open-ended fundamental research.

AI on CPUs

Models of a few megabytes that run on standard processors, with no GPU and no cloud call in the loop. This changes what AI costs and where it can run: on premise, on devices, in embedded hardware.

Explainable by Construction

Every result is a readable structure, traceable to its source, deterministic and reproducible. Where it did not understand, it says where, instead of covering the gap with a fluent answer.

Alignment of LLMs

Act as a small, verifiable layer that checks what large models understood or proposed before anything reaches execution, records or machines: a practical step towards aligned AI.

One core technology, many markets

  • Verifiable agentic AI: Requests to enterprise software turned into exact, checkable actions; large models used only for the hard cases.
  • Regulated industries: Obligations, deadlines and exceptions read exactly from procedures and regulations in pharma, banking, aviation and energy.
  • Verification of large models: Outputs of large language models confirmed, contradicted or flagged as unverifiable, item by item.
  • Assistants that can abstain: Closed-domain assistants on a phone or in a browser, with answers that can be derived and checked.
  • Robotics safety: A small deterministic permission layer between planning and motors, implementable in hardware.
