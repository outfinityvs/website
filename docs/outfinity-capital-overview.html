Outfinity Capital

A first investor-service partnership for technically validated ventures

Outfinity Capital describes the first proposed investor-facing partnership for jointly approved ventures: it identifies suitable investors, aligns opportunities with their mandates, structures individual transactions, coordinates reporting and prepares follow-on financing.

What it is

The first Outfinity Capital partnership presents jointly approved opportunities and structures individual transactions. It will not initially pool investor capital or operate as an investment fund; every investor retains control of each decision.

The Venture Studio performs technical diligence, validates product and market assumptions, structures IP, forms the operating team and defines the execution plan before and after financing.

How the first partnership works

Outfinity supplies a research-backed pipeline, technical due diligence, product and market validation, intellectual property and software foundations, venture design, team formation, and continuing execution support after investment.

A first founding capital partner maps mandates and decision-makers, develops qualified relationships, shapes credible transactions and supports follow-on financing. The role is operational and long-term, not an introduction service.

A repeatable partnership model

The first founding-capital partnership is a starting point, not an exclusive claim on every capital relationship. Outfinity may add complementary specialist, sector, geographic or investor-network partnerships where they create clearer access, stronger execution or better governance for a venture.

Each partnership should have explicit mandates, approval rules, economics and responsibilities. A new partner does not acquire automatic rights over the Venture Studio, existing partnerships or future transactions.

Proof before incorporation

The parties first work under a focused collaboration agreement. The initial Outfinity Capital partnership is incorporated only after tangible proof: normally a signed investment commitment, capital ready to deploy or a completed first financing.

A first 90-day objective

The collaboration maps investors, selects one venture, prepares its case and tests whether the partnership can close financing. A credible commitment then triggers negotiation of the shareholders' agreement, governance, vesting and transaction economics.
