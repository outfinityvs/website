Outfinity Capital

Investor service for technically validated ventures

Outfinity Capital is the proposed investor-facing company for jointly approved ventures: it identifies suitable investors, aligns opportunities with their mandates, structures individual transactions, coordinates reporting and prepares follow-on financing.

What it is

Outfinity Capital presents jointly approved opportunities and structures individual transactions. It will not initially pool investor capital or operate as an investment fund; every investor retains control of each decision.

The Venture Studio performs technical diligence, validates product and market assumptions, structures IP, forms the operating team and defines the execution plan before and after financing.

How the partnership works

Outfinity supplies a research-backed pipeline, technical due diligence, product and market validation, intellectual property and software foundations, venture design, team formation, and continuing execution support after investment.

A founding capital partner maps mandates and decision-makers, develops qualified relationships, shapes credible transactions and supports follow-on financing. The role is operational and long-term, not an introduction service.

Proof before incorporation

The parties first work under a focused collaboration agreement. Outfinity Capital is incorporated only after tangible proof: normally a signed investment commitment, capital ready to deploy or a completed first financing.

A first 90-day objective

The collaboration maps investors, selects one venture, prepares its case and tests whether the partnership can close financing. A credible commitment then triggers negotiation of the shareholders' agreement, governance, vesting and transaction economics.
