How to read this portfolio

Outfinity's current venture set should be read as a portfolio of venture directions rather than as a list of already standardized startups. Some are closer to concrete operating products. Others still sit closer to the research and systems layer from which future products or dedicated companies may emerge.

What connects them is that each direction is being developed against a real structural shift: the rise of agentic work, the need for private and governed AI infrastructure, the demand for AI-native interfaces, the importance of secure communication, and the limits of model-only approaches in scientific or regulated environments.

AssistOS Enterprise

AssistOS Enterprise explores what it means to turn model access into an operational environment. The thesis is that enterprises need governed agentic workspaces, policy-aware execution, file and tool management, and deployment flexibility across local, private, hybrid, and cloud infrastructure.

This direction is relevant to enterprise customers, AI integrators, infrastructure operators, and founder-operators who understand that enterprise AI adoption increasingly depends on system design rather than on raw model novelty.

WebAssist Cloud

WebAssist Cloud turns a company's website into an AI-represented commercial interface that can answer questions, guide serious visitors, qualify leads, and help a website behave more like an informed commercial representative than like a static brochure.

The direction is especially relevant for B2B websites, agencies, CRM integrators, and commercial operators who see conversion and qualification as a growing AI-native surface.

WebMeet AI Collaboration Cloud

WebMeet starts from a simple observation: meetings should become active workspaces rather than passive transcript factories. The venture explores collaborative environments where human participants and specialized agents can work around shared memory, documents, tasks, and decisions in real time.

This direction matters where meetings are part of ongoing technical, research, or operational work rather than isolated communication events.

Elastic Agentic Cloud

Elastic Agentic Cloud addresses the compute reality of the agentic inference economy. It focuses on hosted models, elastic workloads, European data locality, and the cost structure required to support real agentic systems instead of one-off prompt calls.

It becomes more relevant as organizations move from experimentation to workload classes that need predictable budgets, governance, and hosting control.

Ploinky Wormhole Network

Ploinky Wormhole Network explores secure communication for humans, agents, and private workspaces. The thesis is that conventional messaging infrastructure is not sufficient for systems that need controlled routing, cryptographic identity, policy-aware file exchange, and trusted interaction between autonomous actors.

It sits at the intersection of security, collaboration, and AI infrastructure.

Research-originated venture directions

Additional venture directions are being developed around neuro-symbolic systems, executable science, and privacy-preserving collaborative AI. These areas are especially important for trustworthy software, AI for science, and governed cross-organization intelligence workflows.

Those projects may begin closer to the research layer, but they are included because Outfinity is deliberately building a pipeline that can produce both near-term ventures and longer-horizon defensible companies.

Neuro-Symbolic Systems Lab

This venture direction explores architectures that combine language models with symbolic tools, validation logic, and more explicit reasoning structures. It is relevant wherever reliability, inspectability, and governed execution matter more than pure generative fluency.

The opportunity sits at the intersection of trustworthy AI, developer tooling, cybersecurity, and advanced enterprise systems.

Executable Science AI Lab

Executable Science AI Lab turns scientific knowledge into structured digital objects that can be inspected, compared, tested, and used more intelligently by AI systems and research teams.

It is a venture direction aimed at AI for science, reproducibility, evidence mapping, and computational research workflows that need more than paper-level summaries.

Agentic Federated Learning Lab

Agentic Federated Learning Lab explores how organizations can collaborate with AI when they cannot centralize sensitive data, combining local agents, governed workspaces, privacy-aware collaboration, and distributed learning approaches.

The direction is especially relevant in healthcare, pharma, industrial networks, and regulated data environments where shared intelligence must coexist with local control.
