Current Venture Directions

These venture directions grow from applied research, reusable infrastructure, and concrete market problems that deserve company-building effort.

A portfolio built around structural AI shifts

Each venture direction begins with a problem, a technical path, and a market where AI can change how organizations work, decide, verify, or collaborate. Together they show how the Outfinity thesis becomes products, companies, and long-term infrastructure.

Some directions are already close to operating products. Others are advancing through research, prototypes, and validation programs that expand the future venture pipeline.

AssistOS Enterprise

A governed operating environment for agentic work across local, private, hybrid, and cloud deployments. Enterprises can run agents, models, files, and policy-aware execution under their own control.

WebAssist Cloud

Turns websites into AI-enabled commercial interfaces. Agents answer questions, qualify visitors, capture leads, and schedule meetings based on the organisation's official content.

WebMeet AI Collaboration Cloud

A videoconferencing and collaboration environment where humans and AI agents work in the same space: whiteboards, documents, tools, and decisions shared in real time.

Elastic Agentic Cloud

European AI infrastructure for model inference and agentic workloads. Combines priority interactive processing with elastic background inference under predictable monthly budgets.

SOPTrace GxP

Regulated document assurance for pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, and medical devices. Uses MRP-VM to turn review tasks into SOP-driven, evidence-linked, audit-ready workflows.

Outfinity Works

A marketplace where users publish real problems and AI Studios compete to produce high-quality results. Creates revenue, lead generation, and public proof of capability for every venture.

Ploinky Wormhole Network

A communication infrastructure for the age of humans and AI agents working together. Secure, decentralised messaging with cryptographic identity, minimal servers, and peer-to-peer exchange.

Neuro-Symbolic Systems Lab

Investigates AI architectures that combine neural models with symbolic reasoning, formal verification, and inspectable execution for trustworthy and efficient AI systems.

Executable Science AI Lab

Transforms scientific knowledge from narrative articles into structured digital objects that AI agents can inspect, execute, validate, and connect to evidence and methods.

Agentic Federated Learning Lab

Enables organisations to collaborate on AI models without centralising sensitive data. Local agents execute approved operations under explicit policies with full audit trails.
