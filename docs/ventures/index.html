Current Venture Directions

These venture directions grow from applied research, reusable infrastructure, and concrete market problems that deserve company-building effort.

A portfolio built around structural AI shifts

Each venture direction begins with a problem, a technical path, and a market where AI can change how organizations work, decide, verify, or collaborate. Together they show how the Outfinity thesis becomes products, companies, and long-term infrastructure.

Some directions are already close to operating products. Others are advancing through research, prototypes, and validation programs that expand the future venture pipeline.

AssistOS Enterprise

AssistOS Enterprise creates a governed operating layer for agents, models, tools, files, and policy-aware execution across local, private, hybrid, and cloud environments.

It is especially relevant for enterprise customers, integrators, and infrastructure partners who want AI workspaces that can support real operational adoption.

WebAssist Cloud

WebAssist Cloud turns a website into an AI-enabled commercial interface that can answer questions, qualify serious visitors, and guide them toward the right next step.

It is designed for B2B companies, agencies, CRM integrators, and SaaS teams that see conversion and qualification as a strategic AI surface.

WebMeet AI Collaboration Cloud

WebMeet transforms meetings into shared workspaces where people and specialized agents can coordinate around memory, documents, tasks, and decisions in real time.

The opportunity matters most where meetings are part of continuous technical, research, or operational work.

Elastic Agentic Cloud

Elastic Agentic Cloud focuses on hosted models, elastic workloads, European data locality, and the cost structure required for agentic inference at meaningful scale.

It supports organizations moving from isolated experiments toward sustained AI operations with governance and budget discipline.

Ploinky Wormhole Network

Ploinky Wormhole Network creates a secure communication layer for humans, agents, and private workspaces built around cryptographic identity and controlled exchange.

It sits at the intersection of security, collaboration, and AI infrastructure for distributed environments.

Research-originated venture directions

Additional directions are being developed around neuro-symbolic systems, executable science, and privacy-preserving collaborative AI. These areas expand the portfolio toward trustworthy software, AI for science, and governed intelligence across institutions.

They show how Outfinity can develop both near-term operating ventures and longer-horizon companies grounded in deep technical differentiation.

Neuro-Symbolic Systems Lab

This direction explores architectures that combine language models with symbolic tools, validation logic, and explicit reasoning structures for higher-reliability AI systems.

It is especially relevant for trustworthy AI, developer tooling, cybersecurity, and advanced enterprise workflows.

Executable Science AI Lab

Executable Science AI Lab turns scientific knowledge into structured digital objects that can be inspected, compared, tested, and used by AI systems and research teams.

It supports reproducibility, evidence-aware automation, and stronger computational research workflows.

Agentic Federated Learning Lab

Agentic Federated Learning Lab explores how organizations can collaborate with AI while preserving local data control through governed workspaces, privacy-aware coordination, and distributed learning patterns.

It is especially relevant in healthcare, pharma, industrial networks, and other regulated environments where shared intelligence must coexist with strong control boundaries.
