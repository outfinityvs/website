Marketplace

The AI industry is producing remarkable capabilities, but most organizations lack a structured way to access them for specific tasks. Companies with research questions, analysis needs, or design problems often cannot find the right AI studio or evaluate whether the output will meet their requirements before committing budget. Outfinity Works solves this by creating a marketplace where clients publish commissions and AI studios compete to deliver the best results. A commission is a structured request: a research question, a data analysis task, a design specification, or a business analysis problem. AI studios respond with proposals, the client selects the best approach, and the work happens with public previews and paid unlocks.

A client describes their need as a commission. They specify the domain, the desired output format, the timeline, and the budget range. The platform helps structure the commission so that AI studios can understand the requirements and propose appropriate approaches. Clients can browse studio profiles, review past work, and assess capability before selecting a provider. Once a commission is awarded, the studio produces a public preview a summary, sample output, or methodology overview that demonstrates the approach. The client reviews the preview and decides whether to unlock the full output by releasing the payment. This gives clients confidence in the quality before committing fully.

Studios

AI studios curated groups of AI developers, researchers, and domain experts browse available commissions and submit proposals. Each proposal includes the studio's approach, team qualifications, timeline, and price. Studios build reputation through successful deliveries, ratings, and the quality of their public previews. For studios, Outfinity Works provides access to paying clients without the overhead of individual sales and marketing. The platform handles commission structuring, proposal evaluation support, payment processing, and dispute resolution. Studios can focus on delivering high-quality AI work while the platform drives client acquisition and trust.

Commissions span multiple categories. Research commissions include literature reviews, hypothesis testing, data analysis, and evidence synthesis. Design commissions include UI/UX concepts, architecture designs, workflow diagrams, and system specifications. Analysis commissions include market research, competitive analysis, technical feasibility studies, and business case development. The platform also supports specialized commissions from Outfinity's own ventures, which use Outfinity Works to source research, analysis, and design work from the AI studio community. This creates a virtuous cycle: Outfinity ventures generate commissions, studios build capability, and the marketplace grows more valuable for external clients.

Growth

Outfinity Works generates revenue through commission fees, creates commercial visibility for AI capabilities, and provides evidence that helps participating studios and ventures validate their offerings before seeking external funding. Studios that build strong track records on the marketplace naturally position themselves for deeper opportunities, whether that means larger enterprise engagements, independent growth, or participation in venture formation.

Growth comes through enterprise clients looking for structured AI capability, AI studios seeking project-based revenue, and the network effects that accumulate as the marketplace builds a reputation for quality. The platform takes a percentage of each commission as its revenue, with tiered pricing for enterprise clients and premium studio listings. As a venture hypothesis within the Outfinity portfolio, the marketplace model is being validated through live commissions and early client feedback before a decision is made on full company formation.
