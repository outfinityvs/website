WebMeet AI Collaboration Cloud

WebMeet turns meetings into shared work environments where people, documents, and specialized agents can move work forward in real time.

Ventures OverviewAssistOS EnterpriseWebAssist CloudWebMeet AI Collaboration CloudElastic Agentic CloudPloinky Wormhole NetworkNeuro-Symbolic Systems LabExecutable Science AI LabAgentic Federated Learning Lab

Why meetings deserve a richer workspace

Meetings often sit at the center of technical, research, operational, and strategic work. WebMeet is designed to turn that moment into a collaborative environment where memory, documents, decisions, and specialized agents can participate together in context.

This creates stronger continuity between the live conversation and the work that follows, which helps teams preserve insight, connect materials, and make decisions with more support.

What the product direction looks like

WebMeet combines video, audio, chat, shared materials, whiteboard-style interaction, transcripts, summaries, and decision tracking with access to project memory and specialized agents.

Integration with AssistOS and Ploinky gives the meeting a direct path into broader agentic workflows and secure collaboration patterns, making the experience a true work environment.

Where the venture gains traction

The opportunity is strongest in environments where meetings are part of ongoing work and where preserving context creates significant value. That includes research groups, distributed software teams, training environments, customer support organizations, and project-heavy collaboration settings.

As organizations adopt more AI assistance, WebMeet provides a clear path toward AI participation inside the work itself.
