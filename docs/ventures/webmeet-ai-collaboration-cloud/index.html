Venture

WebMeet AI Collaboration Cloud

WebMeet is based on the idea that meetings should become active work environments where humans and specialized AI agents can collaborate in context.

Why meetings need a different model

Most AI meeting tools still behave like post-processing layers. They record, summarize, and occasionally extract actions, but they do not change the meeting itself into a richer workspace. WebMeet starts from a different view: when people meet around technical, research, operational, or strategic work, the meeting should become a place where memory, documents, decisions, and specialized agents can participate in real time.

That shift matters because valuable work often disappears between the meeting and the follow-up. Notes remain disconnected from the materials that produced them, decisions are not linked cleanly to context, and specialized assistance arrives too late to change the conversation while it is happening.

What the product direction looks like

WebMeet combines video, audio, chat, shared materials, whiteboard-style interaction, transcripts, summaries, and decision tracking with access to project memory and specialized agents. The ambition is not to create more meeting ornamentation. It is to give teams a collaboration environment where AI can help inspect documents, prepare artifacts, surface relevant history, and support follow-through while humans remain in control.

Integration with AssistOS and Ploinky matters here because the meeting can become a gateway into deeper agentic workflows rather than an isolated session. That creates a stronger product direction than adding a generic summary feature to an otherwise unchanged call experience.

Where the venture can gain traction

The opportunity is strongest in environments where meetings are part of ongoing work and where the cost of losing context is high. That includes research groups, distributed software teams, training environments, customer support organizations, and project-heavy collaboration settings where decisions need memory and follow-through.

As organizations become more comfortable with AI assistance, the next layer of value is likely to come from AI participation inside the work itself. WebMeet is aimed at that transition.
