Venture

WebAssist Cloud

WebAssist Cloud turns a website into an AI-enabled commercial interface that can answer, qualify, and route serious visitors.

Ventures OverviewAssistOS EnterpriseWebAssist CloudWebMeet AI Collaboration CloudElastic Agentic CloudPloinky Wormhole NetworkNeuro-Symbolic Systems LabExecutable Science AI LabAgentic Federated Learning Lab

Why this venture matters

A large share of B2B websites already contain the information that serious visitors need. WebAssist Cloud helps companies turn that information into an interactive experience that builds confidence, answers practical questions, and guides the visitor toward a meaningful next step.

The venture treats the website as a company surface that can represent offers, positioning, and qualification logic with much more precision and consistency.

What the product enables

The product direction is a hosted SaaS offering that can be embedded into a website with a lightweight installation path. Companies provide the official context that should guide the AI representative, including pages, offers, services, approved explanations, routing logic, and lead-collection rules.

A company-controlled Web Agent Context keeps the AI representative readable, maintainable, and aligned with what the company wants to communicate.

Where the opportunity is strongest

As AI interaction becomes normal, websites increasingly become places where a company is experienced in conversation. WebAssist Cloud is well positioned for B2B businesses, agencies, CRM partners, and SaaS teams that see qualification and conversion as a strategic AI layer.

That creates a path toward a large class of commercial websites that want interactive qualification, guided discovery, and stronger buyer conversations.
