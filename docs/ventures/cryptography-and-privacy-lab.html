Cryptography and Privacy Lab

Foundation

Our team brings extensive hands-on experience from applying and productionizing OpenDSU (opendsu.org) in regulated pharmaceutical environments. OpenDSU provides a secure, traceable data infrastructure deployed across clinical trials, supply chain management, and regulatory compliance workflows in pharma. This real-world experience with GxP-compliant systems, audit trails, and multi-party data collaboration forms the foundation for our approach to privacy-preserving technologies.

We have prepared ideas for next-generation enterprise blockchain systems designed to integrate with AI workloads. Unlike traditional blockchain approaches that struggle with privacy and performance at scale, our architecture combines confidential computing, zero-knowledge proofs, and selective disclosure mechanisms that allow organizations to collaborate without exposing sensitive business logic or data. These systems support complex smart contracts that can execute AI inference, aggregate insights across participants, and enforce data governance policies at the protocol level.

Technology

Our federated learning approach goes beyond traditional model aggregation. Drawing from the Achilles project, we introduce AI agents that operate within each participant's trusted environment. These agents execute sophisticated analysis tasks, enforce export policies, and participate in collaborative workflows while keeping sensitive data under local control. They enable collaborative intelligence that includes not just model training but also analysis, knowledge extraction, and decision support.

For managing highly sensitive data such as genetic information, financial records, or healthcare data, we explore neuro-symbolic architectures like Vector Symbolic Architectures (VSA) and Hyperdimensional Computing (HDC). These approaches allow computation on encrypted or obfuscated data representations, enabling analysis without exposing the underlying sensitive information. We are also developing advanced Differential Privacy mechanisms with context-aware privacy budgets and adaptive guarantees that adjust based on query sensitivity.

Direction

The venture path leads toward a platform that enables governed AI collaboration across organizational boundaries, with particular strength in pharma, healthcare, and financial services. Initial customers are likely to be research consortia, hospital networks, pharmaceutical companies, and industry groups that need shared intelligence without centralized data. Revenue comes from platform subscriptions, deployment services, and consulting for organizations setting up privacy-preserving collaboration networks.

Our deep pharma experience with OpenDSU provides immediate credibility and a clear path to early customers in regulated industries that value proven expertise combined with cutting-edge technology.
