Venture

Elastic Agentic Cloud

Elastic Agentic Cloud supports the hosted infrastructure, workload orchestration, and data-locality requirements that make agentic systems economically workable.

Ventures OverviewAssistOS EnterpriseWebAssist CloudWebMeet AI Collaboration CloudElastic Agentic CloudPloinky Wormhole NetworkNeuro-Symbolic Systems LabExecutable Science AI LabAgentic Federated Learning Lab

Why the infrastructure layer matters

Agentic systems create a broader mix of workloads across planning, analysis, verification, document work, and background execution. Elastic Agentic Cloud is built to support that fuller operating reality.

The venture organizes interactive inference and elastic background inference into a more practical operational model for organizations running agent-heavy systems.

What the product direction involves

The direction includes European-hosted access to open-weight or licensed models, private endpoints, and runtime environments suitable for hosted agentic systems. It also emphasizes workload classes and queueing logic that help organizations reason about cost, delay, and hosting policy with more precision.

This becomes especially important when organizations need data locality, predictable budget behavior, or a smoother path between experimentation and production.

Why it matters strategically

For enterprises and AI product teams operating in Europe, infrastructure questions are increasingly shaped by jurisdiction, trust, and control together with raw performance. Elastic Agentic Cloud creates a hosting and execution environment that makes agentic AI more usable under these real operational conditions.

That gives investors, cloud and GPU partners, and enterprise customers a concrete way to support the next phase of AI value creation.
