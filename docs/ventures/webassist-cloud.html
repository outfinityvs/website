Product

Most B2B websites contain the detailed information that serious buyers need: product specifications, pricing logic, compliance certifications, integration guides, and case studies. But that information is embedded in static pages that cannot answer questions, qualify interest, or guide a visitor toward the right next step. WebAssist Cloud treats the website as an AI-represented commercial surface. Instead of forcing visitors to navigate menus and search bars, the product lets them ask questions in natural language and receive accurate, company-approved answers that build confidence and move the conversation forward.

The product is a hosted SaaS offering that embeds into any website with a lightweight installation path. Companies provide the official context that should guide the AI representative: pages, offers, services, approved explanations, routing logic, and lead-collection rules. A company-controlled Web Agent Context keeps the AI readable, maintainable, and aligned with what the company wants to communicate. The AI does not guess or hallucinate answers beyond its configured context. Every response is grounded in the materials the company supplies, which means the commercial conversation stays accurate and on-brand. Updates to the context propagate immediately without code changes or redeployment.

Intelligence

WebAssist Cloud does more than answer questions. It qualifies visitors by asking structured questions, capturing intent signals, and routing serious prospects to the right sales channel, partner, or content path. The qualification logic is configurable and can reflect the company's own sales process and lead scoring criteria. This turns the website from a passive information resource into an active commercial funnel. Companies capture richer lead data, reduce time spent on unqualified inquiries, and ensure that high-intent visitors receive immediate attention rather than filling out a form and waiting.

For B2B companies, the return comes in several forms: reduced burden on sales and support teams, higher conversion rates from qualified traffic, and better data about what serious visitors actually need. WebAssist Cloud captures the questions, concerns, and priorities that real buyers express, which feeds back into product positioning and content strategy. The product also reduces friction for the buyer. Instead of scheduling a demo to get basic questions answered, a prospect can resolve their initial curiosity immediately and arrive at a human conversation already informed and qualified. This compresses the sales cycle.

Deployment

WebAssist Cloud integrates with common CRM (Customer Relationship Management) platforms, marketing automation tools, and analytics systems. Lead data, conversation transcripts, and qualification outcomes are seamlessly integrated into and synchronised with the company's existing workflows. The installation footprint is minimal: a JavaScript snippet, a configuration panel, and a context management interface. The platform supports multiple deployment models depending on the company's security and compliance requirements. A standard cloud deployment works for most B2B sites, while private instances are available for organisations that require data residency or greater control over AI processing.

The primary market includes B2B businesses with complex offerings that require explanation and qualification. Web agencies and CRM integrators can offer WebAssist Cloud as a value-added service for their clients. SaaS teams benefit from an AI layer that reduces support tickets and improves trial-to-paid conversion. As AI interaction becomes a normal expectation on commercial websites, the market for intelligent qualification and guided discovery will grow significantly. WebAssist Cloud is positioned to capture that transition with a focused product that solves a concrete commercial problem.
