03 / ScriptaHub

If everyone can write a book, how will the right books find their readers?

Open collaboration for niche books

Generative AI is making long-form content easier to produce, but production is only half the challenge. Serious, specialized books still need editorial quality, sustained collaboration, trustworthy attribution and distribution to the right communities. ScriptaHub explores a model inspired by open-source software: authors and contributors could version, review, extend and fork manuscripts while building living books and a discoverable library of niche knowledge.

VisitScriptaHubExploring a GitHub-inspired approach to writing, revision and discovery, where authors and experts collaborate and quality work can reach its readers.scriptahub.com ↗

04 / Bizanso

Reclaim the internet’s promise: connect people to meaningful opportunities.

AI matching that works for the individual

Finding the right collaborator, employer, co-founder or project is still surprisingly inefficient, and existing profiling systems often optimize for the platform’s engagement or transaction model. Bizanso explores individualized AI matching across people, projects and opportunities, with explicit preferences and meaningful control retained by the participants. It is not a claim that intermediaries disappear; it is a test of whether their role can be renegotiated.

VisitBizansoTesting people-first discovery of collaborators, projects and opportunities, where AI personalizes matches without taking control away from the participants.bizanso.com ↗

05 / Deep Proof

For consequential decisions, a chat answer is not a review process.

High-stakes AI analysis with a method

Consequential assessments require defined criteria, dependable coverage, explicit evidence and repeatable testing. Deep Proof pursues an engineered, neuro-symbolic approach to dossier analysis: a documented review method supported by model-based analysis, structured checks and validation suites. Its underlying platform is open source; professional value comes from building and calibrating specialized AI Review Skills and integrating them into real decision workflows.

VisitDeep ProofBuilding professional, testable AI Review Skills for complex dossiers and consequential decisions: explicit criteria, traceable evidence and human judgment.deepproof.ch ↗

06 / AssistOS Enterprise

AI workers need roles, tools, context and supervision, not just prompts.

A workplace for virtual employees

Virtual employees may become an important layer of enterprise operations, but useful autonomy requires more than a language model interface. Built around the open-source AssistOS ecosystem, AssistOS Enterprise explores persistent AI workers that act in governed workspaces, use tools, follow workflows and maintain task context under human oversight. The commercial opportunity lies in configuring these workers around specific business processes and connecting them to existing systems.

Outfinity Venture Studio

AI makes building faster. We make venture formation more deliberate.

Outfinity is a Switzerland-based venture validation studio connecting advanced research, entrepreneurial ideas and people capable of bringing them into the world. Our work starts before incorporation: with a thesis worth testing, an evidence plan and a founding team that can earn the right to build.

Where research becomes a venture worth building

Outfinity combines deep-tech research, market validation and venture-building discipline. We bring founders, researchers, operators and strategic partners together to test promising ideas, design viable businesses and organize their formation in Switzerland and beyond.

Why our approach matters

The cost of prototyping is falling. The cost of earning trust, finding distribution, proving scientific claims and changing the way organizations work is not. We focus on these harder constraints—before major commitments are made.

What the studio does

We source opportunities, pressure-test assumptions, validate technology and demand, structure collaborations and intellectual property, organize founding teams and partnerships, and prepare ventures for launch or financing. A clear “not yet” or “no” is a legitimate validation outcome.

What we are actually trying to validate

01 / Venture Studio — Build after the evidence, not before it

Outfinity applies venture-studio methods to opportunities arising from scientific research, emerging technologies and under-served markets. Instead of starting from the assumption that every promising prototype should become a startup, we connect the technical thesis with the customer, the business model, the team and the capital structure. That makes validation itself a core product of the studio.

What we are validating: Whether a differentiated capability meets a consequential need, whether buyers or users will act, and whether the right founders and partners can sustain an independent venture.

Outfinity’s role: Opportunity sourcing, research and technical diligence, customer discovery, venture design, co-founder search, partnership and IP structuring, initial capitalization and spin-out preparation.

02 / Axiologic AI Lab — A new Swiss lab for a new approach to machine understanding

Recent research at Axiologic suggests a potentially important direction in automated understanding, including work associated with the VSS (VSA Semantic Space) approach. Outfinity is bringing together prospective founders and strategic partners for an Axiologic AI Lab in Switzerland. The proposed lab is intended to assume and further develop the research activities of Axiologic Research in Romania, subject to a clear legal, organizational and intellectual-property framework.

What we are validating: Independent reproducibility, rigorous benchmarks against meaningful baselines, technical scalability, commercial applications and credible commitments from co-founders and research partners.

Outfinity’s role: Organize the founders consortium, establish the research and governance plan, coordinate technology and IP arrangements, secure partners, and design the Swiss lab’s institutional and venture pathway.

03 / ScriptaHub — If everyone can write a book, how will the right books find their readers?

Generative AI is making long-form content easier to produce, but production is only half the challenge. Serious, specialized books still need editorial quality, sustained collaboration, trustworthy attribution and distribution to the right communities. ScriptaHub explores a model inspired by open-source software: authors and contributors could version, review, extend and fork manuscripts while building living books and a discoverable library of niche knowledge.

What we are validating: Whether contributors will collaborate around books as they do around software, whether curation can protect quality, and whether readers and expert communities create a sustainable path to discovery and revenue.

Outfinity’s role: Organize product and editorial experiments, early author communities, contribution governance, distribution partnerships and the venture’s economic model.

04 / Bizanso — Reclaim the internet’s original promise: connect people to meaningful opportunities

The internet can connect almost anyone, yet finding the right collaborator, employer, co-founder or project is still surprisingly inefficient. Existing profiling systems often optimize for the platform’s engagement or transaction model. Bizanso explores an alternative: individualized AI matching across people, projects and opportunities, with explicit preferences and meaningful control retained by the participants. It is not a claim that intermediaries disappear; it is a test of whether their role can be renegotiated.

What we are validating: Match relevance and reciprocity, privacy and user agency, trust in recommendations, and whether a people-aligned intermediary can sustain a viable business model.

Outfinity’s role: Develop the matching hypothesis, organize pilots with target communities, validate onboarding and discovery mechanics, and shape governance, monetization and partnerships.

05 / Deep Proof — For consequential decisions, a chat answer is not a review process

General-purpose AI chat is useful for exploring documents, but consequential assessments require defined criteria, dependable coverage, explicit evidence and repeatable testing. Deep Proof pursues an engineered, neuro-symbolic approach to dossier analysis: a documented review method supported by model-based analysis, structured checks and validation suites. Its underlying platform is open source; professional value comes from building and calibrating specialized AI Review Skills and integrating them into real decision workflows.

What we are validating: Whether tailored and rigorously evaluated review skills improve coverage, traceability and consistency over ad hoc document chat, and whether organizations will pay for that assurance.

Outfinity’s role: Provide domain analysis, skill engineering, benchmark and test design, expert calibration, enterprise integration and stewardship. The AI prepares and documents the analysis; people retain the final decision.

06 / AssistOS Enterprise — AI workers need roles, tools, context and supervision, not just prompts

Virtual employees may become an important layer of enterprise operations, but useful autonomy requires more than a language model interface. Built around the open-source AssistOS ecosystem, AssistOS Enterprise explores persistent AI workers that can act in governed workspaces, use tools, follow workflows and maintain task context under human oversight. The commercial opportunity lies in configuring these workers around specific business processes and connecting them to an organization’s existing systems.

What we are validating: Which workflows deliver measurable value, what degrees of autonomy are acceptable, and which skills, integrations, security controls and operating practices make adoption repeatable.

Outfinity’s role: Build domain-specific skills, customize agent packages and workflows, integrate enterprise systems, design permissions and deployment, and support evaluation, monitoring and ongoing improvement.

The laboratory, products and venture structures are described as propositions under organization and validation. Scientific claims are framed as research hypotheses until independently reproduced; commercial value is presented as a thesis until market evidence exists.
