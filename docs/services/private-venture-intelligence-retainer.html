Outfinity Venture Studio (www.outfinity.ch)

What It Delivers

The Private Venture Intelligence Retainer is the highest-trust engagement Outfinity offers. It is a recurring quarterly or annual subscription that combines periodic written research, private discussion sessions, selective documentation access, and early visibility into the venture directions Outfinity is actively researching and shaping. Retainer clients also receive the right to request a defined number of targeted evaluations per year, functioning as mini Venture Exploration Memos scoped to questions the client selects. This is a paid research access and advisory relationship, not a fundraising vehicle, not an investment club, and not a promise of deal flow. The client pays for professional research capacity, structured information delivery, and selective visibility into a venture studio's exploration pipeline.

The engagement typically includes quarterly or monthly written briefs covering AI, blockchain, privacy, agentic systems, and regulated AI venture directions that Outfinity is actively researching, each providing technical context, market signals, and maturity assessment. Regular private discussion sessions of 60–90 minutes allow the client to review findings, ask questions, and connect Outfinity's research to their own investment thesis. Selective documentation access gives the client visibility into venture exploration outputs, technical architecture documents, and validation findings that are relevant to their stated interests, subject to confidentiality and third-party obligations. Retainer clients who qualify may also be invited into the paid tier of the Explorer Circle, gaining access to the working network of researchers, builders, experts, and co-investors who shape opportunities before incorporation.

Engagement and Scope

The retainer is designed for multi-family offices that want to offer AI and deep-tech intelligence to their client families, single family offices building a long-term AI investment thesis, corporate venture units that need ongoing technical visibility into emerging AI directions, and strategic angel groups that invest regularly in the space. It is not designed for passive capital allocators who expect a standard fund product; it works best for investors who are technically curious and want to develop their own conviction through structured research access over time. A standard annual retainer typically includes quarterly memos and sessions, selective documentation access, and two to four targeted evaluations per year. Pricing starts at 25k CHF per year and scales with the depth of access, the frequency of deliverables, and the number of targeted evaluations included. Every engagement begins with an alignment session to define the client's technology interests, investment thesis, risk priorities, and the specific research themes that will guide the retainer period. All deliverables and access rights are defined contractually, with clear boundaries between the service relationship and any future investment discussion, which is handled through separate legal documents if and when the opportunity matures to that stage.
