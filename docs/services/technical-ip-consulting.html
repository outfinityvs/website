Intellectual Property Evaluation

Understanding the true value and structural soundness of a software asset is crucial for both startups and non-technical companies. We provide comprehensive review, technical analysis, and intellectual property evaluation services to clarify what you own and how it is built. This ensures that your intellectual property is technically robust, scalable, and built on modern principles, reducing the risk of hidden technical debt and future liabilities.

Modernization and Refactoring Estimation

In addition to intellectual property evaluation, we offer precise cost estimation for refactoring and application modernization. By leveraging specialized teams that use AI coding agents and specification-driven development methods, we deliver clear, realistic budgets and timelines for rebuilding or modernizing legacy applications. This approach significantly reduces overhead and provides a predictable path to a modern software architecture, empowering your business to scale with confidence.
