GxP Software Qualification

Developing software in highly regulated environments requires a specialized approach to compliance and architecture. We have extensive experience working with large pharmaceutical and life sciences companies, including Novartis, MSD, and GSK. We understand the stringent demands of these industries and offer dedicated GxP software qualification services to ensure that your technical solutions meet all necessary regulatory standards from day one.

AI-Enabled Pipelines and Architectures

We focus on creating custom solutions and specific architectures that balance innovation with strict compliance. Furthermore, we design and implement development pipelines integrated with AI agents. This allows your internal teams to safely and effectively leverage agentic technologies in their research and development processes, accelerating innovation while maintaining a fully traceable and compliant development lifecycle.
