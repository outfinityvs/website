Consolidating Teams Through Research

Entrepreneurship can be significantly supported, especially in its initial phase, by research projects. While the competition is high and bureaucracy can be challenging, research grants offer a valuable, non-dilutive funding source that allows a technical team to consolidate and focus on innovation. We strongly encourage partnerships that begin with collaborative research project writing, and we actively help teams navigate this complex landscape to secure the funding they need.

Building Consortiums and Spin-offs

We provide comprehensive consortium building and European research project proposal writing services specifically tailored for Swiss companies. These initiatives can also serve as the foundation for spin-offs from established companies facing challenges and seeking advanced AI or blockchain solutions. We provide deep technical insights, real-world validation, and support in creating a venture focused entirely on solving an important, scalable problem. This approach not only solves immediate technical needs but also paves the way for long-term venture building.
