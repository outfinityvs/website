Enterprise Blockchain, Not Cryptocurrencies

Our focus is exclusively on enterprise blockchain and privacy-preserving technologies. We are not interested in cryptocurrencies or speculative token markets. Our work addresses a fundamentally different problem: how organizations can transform the quality, integrity, and confidentiality of their data while solving the privacy and trust challenges that arise in multi-party business environments. Companies today face growing pressure from data protection regulation, cross-border compliance requirements, and the need to collaborate with partners, suppliers, and customers without exposing sensitive business logic or proprietary information. Enterprise blockchain and privacy-preserving computation provide the technical foundation to address these challenges at scale.

Research Background and OpenDSU

Our capability in this area is grounded in extensive experience with European research projects and, through Axiologic Research, in our role as coordinators of the OpenDSU ecosystem. OpenDSU provides a secure, traceable data infrastructure that has been applied and productionized in regulated pharmaceutical environments, including clinical trials, supply chain management, and regulatory compliance workflows. This hands-on experience with GxP-compliant systems, audit trails, and multi-party data collaboration gives us a rare combination of deep technical knowledge and real-world deployment credibility. We have developed next-generation enterprise blockchain architectures designed to integrate with AI workloads, combining confidential computing, zero-knowledge proofs, and selective disclosure mechanisms that allow organizations to collaborate without centralizing sensitive data.

What We Offer

We help organizations evaluate, design, and implement privacy-preserving solutions tailored to their specific data governance challenges. This includes architecture design for enterprise blockchain systems, federated learning pipelines that keep data under local control, confidential computing strategies, and differential privacy frameworks. Our consulting draws on active research through the Cryptography and Privacy Lab and the Genetic Data AI Lab, where we develop advanced methods for privacy-preserving computation, neuro-symbolic approaches to sensitive data management, and hyperdimensional computing techniques for high-dimensional datasets. Whether the challenge is pharmaceutical data sharing, financial services compliance, healthcare collaboration, or industrial supply chain integrity, we bring the technical depth and research background to design solutions that are both principled and practical.
