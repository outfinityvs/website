Current website setup

At present, Outfinity’s public site is served as static content and does not rely on analytics scripts or marketing trackers to function.

We do not set required cookies for core content delivery beyond what browsers apply by default.

External form processing

Contact actions use an external Google Form URL. Google may apply session or functionality cookies in the Google domain as part of standard form processing.

Any cookies connected to the form flow are governed by Google’s own policies.

Visitor controls

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser for our site and third-party domains. If you prefer not to use the browser form flow, please request an alternative channel by contacting us.

Change management

When website technologies change, this page is updated before or at rollout as part of the internal publishing procedure.
