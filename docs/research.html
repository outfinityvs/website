Research and Technical Base

Foundation

Applied research helps Outfinity identify structural opportunities, develop technical conviction, and build assets that can later become product foundations, infrastructure modules, or company-level differentiators. This gives the studio a deeper basis for venture creation and a better ability to choose opportunities that can sustain long-term advantage.

Axiologic Research contributes a significant part of this technical grounding, together with systems such as AssistOS, Ploinky, MRP-VM, OpenDSU, and related work on agentic workspaces, secure communication, explicit execution, and governed data handling. Because this base compounds across several venture directions, Outfinity can move faster on new opportunities and build companies with deeper architectural continuity.

Open-Source Technologies and Public Research

A substantial part of the technical base behind Outfinity ventures is publicly documented and, where possible, open source. We share research articles, architectural proposals, reference implementations, and technical ideas across two main public sites. These are not marketing pages, they are working research spaces where we develop arguments, publish preprints, document prototypes, and occasionally share ideas that have not yet found their venture form.

AssistOS is an open-source ecosystem for building, deploying, and managing advanced AI agents, developed by Axiologic Research as part of the European Achilles research project. The site documents the full AssistOS toolchain: AchillesAgentLib for cognitive agent architecture, AGISystem2 for logical reasoning and formal verification, Ploinky for secure sandboxed deployment, Achilles IDE and Achilles CLI for developer automation, and AchillesCopilotBasicSkills for core agent capabilities. The vision documented on the site is that AI assistants should adapt to users, blending natural language with structured forms, version control, IDEs, and precise input, rather than forcing everything into a chat interface. A second core idea is specification-driven R&D: AI as a compiler from human specifications to artefacts, whether code, diagrams, research articles, or creative works. The site also covers our research into trustworthy AI, including neuro-symbolic solutions for opacity, bias, and hallucination in regulated industries, and an ongoing evaluation of open-source agents for integration into the AssistOS secure workspace model. AssistOS directly underpins ventures such as AssistOS Enterprise, Ploinky Wormhole Network, and SOPTrace GxP.

Our Neuro-symbolic Scientific Blog is a research site dedicated to System 2 engineering for reliable AI. The central research program is Meta-Rational Pragmatics (MRP), an article series of 17 published articles that develops the conceptual, architectural, and implementation layers of MRP-VM, a runtime for goals, frames, routes, interpreters, and auditable execution. MRP-VM is not a classical virtual machine. It is a control layer that orchestrates internal commands and external interpreters under explicit policies, moving part of intelligence out of opaque model inference and into a form that is easier to control, cheaper to run, and more realistic to deploy on ordinary hardware. The site also covers Ruliology, exploratory research into abstract rule spaces and inductive bias discovery for neuro-symbolic AI, as well as methodologies for AI-assisted research, a structured research themes map covering verification, symbolic methods, VSA/HDC, world models, small models, and CPU-oriented ML, and a collection of public experiments with open-source prototypes and reference implementations. This body of work directly informs ventures such as Neuro-Symbolic Systems Lab, Executable Science AI Lab, and AssistOS Enterprise.

How Research Connects to Venture Directions

The ventures proposed under Current Venture Directions are not independent ideas that happen to share a brand. They are expressions of a coherent technical base that compounds across multiple directions. The connections are concrete.

Enterprise AI

AssistOS Enterprise

Draws directly from the agent architecture, specification-driven R&D, and trustworthy AI research documented on assistos.org. The governed execution layer, skill contracts, and agentic orchestration patterns developed in the Achilles project become the product foundation for enterprise deployments.

Secure Communication

Ploinky Wormhole Network

Originates from the Ploinky component of the AssistOS toolchain, a secure communication and sandboxed deployment layer that enables agents to operate in controlled, peer-to-peer environments with cryptographic identity and minimal server infrastructure.

Neuro-symbolic AI

Neuro-Symbolic Systems Lab

Rooted in the MRP and MRP-VM research published on agisystem2.com. The regime selection architecture, typed intermediates, executable natural language, and the broader argument that useful intelligence depends on governed coordination of multiple execution regimes rather than one universal model.

Scientific AI

Executable Science AI Lab

Extends the MRP-VM runtime toward research automation: literature work, synthesis, protocol handling, and structured inquiry under stronger epistemic control. The same runtime discipline that improves agent reliability in enterprise settings applies directly to scientific workflows where auditability and provenance are non-negotiable.

Privacy & Blockchain

Cryptography and Privacy Lab

Builds on deep experience with OpenDSU in regulated pharmaceutical environments, combined with privacy-preserving computation patterns. Enterprise blockchain, federated learning via Achilles agents, and neuro-symbolic architectures for private data management form the technical nucleus.

Genomic Data

Genetic Data AI Lab

Draws from the VSA/HDC research themes mapped on agisystem2.com, hyperdimensional computing and vector symbolic architectures that allow computation on encrypted or obfuscated data representations, relevant for genomic datasets where privacy guarantees must be maintained.

Regulated AI

SOPTrace GxP

Uses the MRP-VM execution model and specification-driven approach to create evidence-linked, SOP-driven review workflows in pharma and biotech, where bounded, auditable agent behaviour is a regulatory requirement, not a feature.

Commercial & Infrastructure

WebAssist, WebMeet, Elastic Agentic Cloud

Apply the agentic orchestration, skill-based architecture, and local execution principles from the research base to commercial interfaces, real-time collaboration, and European AI infrastructure respectively.

Creative & Marketplace

SCRIPTA & Outfinity Works

Extend the specification-driven R&D vision, AI as a compiler from human intent to structured artefacts, into creative production and marketplace models.

Each venture traces back to specific research objects, published articles, open-source components, or architectural patterns documented across the technical base. Investors and partners who want to evaluate the depth behind a venture direction can follow these connections from venture page to research article to reference implementation.

Translation

Research becomes venture material when it is connected to a real problem, a credible deployment path, the right collaborators, and a structure that supports commercialization. Outfinity creates that bridge through thesis design, validation, partner alignment, and company formation. This allows the studio to support both near-term product ventures and longer-horizon companies rooted in serious technical depth.

The public research sites, the open-source components, and the published article series are not separate from the venture pipeline. They are the pipeline's upstream. Investors and partners who want to understand the technical basis of a venture direction can trace it back through these materials, from venture page, to research article, to reference implementation, to the underlying architectural argument.
