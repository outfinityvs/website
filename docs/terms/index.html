Terms

These terms describe how website visitors and Outfinity interact around content, contact requests, and project-specific agreements.

Website use

Visitors use this website to understand Outfinity's venture-building model, venture directions, and collaboration themes. Website content supports discovery, orientation, and first contact around relevant opportunities.

Use of the site, its content, and any submitted information is expected to respect applicable law, intellectual property, and the practical operating boundaries of the website.

Project-specific agreements

Collaborations, paid work, advisory roles, investments, venture participation, and other structured relationships are governed by separate written agreements tailored to the specific opportunity.

These agreements define the rights, responsibilities, economics, and working terms that apply once the relationship moves beyond public website content.

Intellectual property and conduct

Website content, branding, and published materials remain subject to the intellectual-property rights attached to them. Visitors are expected to use the site lawfully and in a way that respects those rights and the integrity of the website experience.

Where a relationship develops into active collaboration, the relevant project documents define the operative rights and obligations.
