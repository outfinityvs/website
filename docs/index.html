Outfinity Venture Studio

Research-backed AI venture formation.

Outfinity builds venture paths around useful, local, composable AI systems. The studio works where applied research, technical infrastructure, venture design, and qualified partners need to come together before a company is ready to stand alone.

Its venture directions grow out of applied research developed with Axiologic Research and from infrastructure behind AssistOS, Ploinky, MRP-VM, OpenDSU, agentic workspaces, secure collaboration, and neuro-symbolic systems. The aim is not to manufacture startup noise. It is to build ventures that become stronger as the technical and commercial story becomes clearer.

Research to venture

Built on a compounding technical base

Outfinity links venture formation to reusable systems and applied research rather than treating each opportunity as a fresh narrative exercise.

That is why the studio can explore both near-term enterprise opportunities and longer-horizon venture directions without losing architectural continuity.
