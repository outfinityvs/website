For AI and Research Audiences

Useful, local, composable AI.

We bet that the future of AI will not be dystopian and controlled by a few entities. It will be built brick by brick through genuinely useful applications, thousands of specialized models, and neuro-symbolic systems that combine learned capabilities with explicit structure.

01

No single model wins every problem.

The No Free Lunch theorem is a useful reminder: intelligence will not become a universal silicon god. Practical AI needs specialization, routing, validation, and domain structure.

02

Agentic AI needs a harness.

Our direction is Meta Rational Pragmatics: custom, domain-specific agent harnesses that make interpretation, decomposition, execution, and review more explicit.

03

Research automation needs control.

AI-assisted research must support literature work, synthesis, protocols, and structured inquiry under stronger epistemic and operational discipline.
