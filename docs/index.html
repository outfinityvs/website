Outfinity Venture Studio

Building AI ventures with researchers, AI Labs, entrepreneurs, experts and investors.

Outfinity exists to turn strong technical insight into companies with clear market relevance, disciplined structure, and room to scale. We connect research, software architecture, industry expertise, entrepreneurship and investment discipline to create ventures that matter.

The studio validates opportunities before creating companies, protects intellectual property through clear agreements, and launches autonomous ventures only when there is a credible path to product, market and financing. We do not launch companies prematurely.

Positioning

Why Outfinity Is Different

Outfinity is not a classical accelerator, a consultancy, a passive investor, or a closed corporate lab. It is a lean venture-building platform designed for AI opportunities that need thesis design, technical validation, partner matching, and disciplined company formation before they seek capital.

We combine the thesis discipline of a venture studio, the technical depth of an AI research environment, and the commercial focus of an entrepreneurial team all in one structured path.
