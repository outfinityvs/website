This page explains how public website information relates to formal Outfinity collaborations, investments, and venture documents.

Public information

This website presents Outfinity's studio model, venture directions, and collaboration themes in a public format designed for general understanding.

It helps visitors understand the opportunity areas, the partner profiles, and the venture-building approach that shape the Outfinity ecosystem.

Formal documentation

Investment terms, collaboration rights, service scopes, company arrangements, and other structured relationships are documented separately for each opportunity and jurisdiction.

That approach gives every relationship clear written terms matched to the actual venture, the actual participants, and the actual stage of maturity.

No public offering

Nothing on this website is intended as an offer to sell securities, a solicitation of investment, or professional legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice.

Any transaction is considered only through the documents, processes, and legal framework applicable to the specific situation.
