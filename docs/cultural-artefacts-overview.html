Outfinity Cultural Artefacts

Outfinity Cultural Artefacts is a reading library for the institutional, technical and cultural thesis behind Outfinity Venture Studio. Its five main books ask how a venture studio can build category brands, shared infrastructure and decentralised cooperation without becoming another closed platform.

Investing in an AI-Dominated Economy

The Outfinity Investment Thesis argues that durable AI ventures need more than access to models. It sets out the research, governed execution, rights, reputation, regulation, distribution and ecosystem logic that can support independent positions, and explains how Metacult, Outfinitism and decentralised brands shape Outfinity's venture strategy.

Metacult

Modernizing Humanity's Oldest Social Technology: How to Build Collective Power Without Crushing the Individual examines how collective identity, memory, membership and shared purpose can be made more conscious, humane and corrigible in an AI-shaped society.

Decentralised Brands

Shared Identity, Distributed Power, and the Architecture of Resilient Cooperation investigates how an identity, promise and reputation can be carried by autonomous organisations without becoming the private sovereignty of one company or an undefended commons.

The Licence and the Shared Name

From GPL and Linux to Constitutional Compacts for Decentralised Brands separates the historical record of free and open-source software from a prospective research architecture for brand custody, contribution governance, provenance, forks and rights.

The Captured Internet

Human Nature, Algorithmic Power, and the Architecture of a More Governable Network considers how technically efficient networks can still narrow human agency, and investigates alternatives compatible with open discovery, collaboration, culture and economic participation.
