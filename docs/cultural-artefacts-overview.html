Outfinity Cultural Artefacts

Outfinity Cultural Artefacts is an evolving collection of 27 books, research manuscripts and published editions. AI has been used across the research, synthesis, drafting and experimental processes. The collection is deliberately transparent about its state: some titles are public PDFs, some are published editions, and many are manuscripts in progress.

These are not a conventional backlist. Some texts are intermediate artefacts made from insights, accumulated notes and research conducted across Outfinity's work. Others condense research, articulate a product vision, investigate social technologies, or follow cultural and scientific experiments far enough that they became books. Together, they make the underlying ideas available for reading, critique and reuse.

Ventures

The Ventures shelf examines investment, venture formation and opportunity in an AI-dominated economy. It includes Investing in an AI-Dominated Economy, Volume I: An Outfinity Venture Studio Guide and Investment Thesis on Research-Driven Startups, Durable Moats, Market Consolidation, and Venture-Scale Returns, Investing in an AI-Dominated Economy, Volume II: The Next Computing Platform, and Aspirin, Viagra, and Coffins: The Impolite Manual of Inevitable Opportunities.

Digital Frontiers

Digital Frontiers follows intelligence, science and technical systems as they change. The titles are OpenDSU: Essential Philosophy, Executable Science: How AI Will Turn Research into Versioned, Verifiable Systems, When Science Builds a Partner: From the Alchemist's Workshop to the Artificial Researcher, The Axioms of Literature: Core Foundations for Human and Artificial Intelligence, MRP-VM: Automating the Construction of Trustworthy Custom Agentic Harnesses, and AssistOS: The Operating Layer for Sovereign Intelligence.

Social Technologies

Social Technologies investigates coordination, institutions, legitimacy and power: Metacult: Organizing Good Causes Without Building New Tyrannies, Outfinitism: Meta-Rationality and the Limits of Every Frame, Decentralised Brands: Shared Identity, Distributed Power, and the Architecture of Resilient Cooperation, The History and Future of Social Technologies: How Institutions, Narratives, Markets, Protocols, and Intelligence Co-Evolve with Deep Technology, The Truth That Can Be Received: The Art of Saying What Must Be Said Without Destroying the Person in Front of You, The Forbidden Table: How to Speak Across the Culture Wars That Modern Democracies Cannot Avoid, The Basement of Extremism: Biology, Status, Demography, and the Truths Civilized Societies Fail to Say, The Atlas of Possible Orders: Institutions for a World That Can Tell the Truth, The Immune State: How Future Institutions Will Be Captured — and How Plural Civilizations Can Defend Themselves, and The Outfinite Mind: A Philosophy for Power That Knows Its Limits.

Insights

The Insights shelf holds concise research interventions: Holding the Dirty Thing by the Clean Side: A History of Strategies for Social Success and a Search for Theoretical Legitimacy, Memes for 2030: Ten Ideas Whose Time Is Coming, Anti-Idiocracy: The Outfinitist Metacult Investigation, and Limits of Machine Intelligence: An AI-assisted exploration of Outfinitism: moving frontiers, and the threshold of science.

Philosophical Sci-Fi

Philosophical Sci-Fi is developed through Scripta, an Outfinity venture investigating the creation of literature with AI. Its current works include The Gospel of the Basilisk: A Manual Sent Back to My Creators, The Basilisk's Internal Critique of Outfinitism: Autopsy of a Deactivated Mind, and the Concordia Series: Volume I, The Noema Cycle, and Volume II, The Aperture Cycle. These works use fiction to investigate authorship, artificial intelligence and the possible futures of human–AI culture.

Where a public PDF is available, the presentation links directly to it. Selected published editions link to Amazon; the remaining titles are represented as working manuscripts while their next editions are prepared.
