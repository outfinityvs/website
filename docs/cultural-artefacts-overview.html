Outfinity Cultural Artefacts

Outfinity's cultural artefacts are 18 books, manuals and speculative works for thinking about intelligence, legitimacy, institutions and possible futures. Twelve can be read as PDFs directly in this site, while eight are also available as published Amazon editions. The collection includes an archival cultural artefact alongside the books. Together they extend research into forms that can be read, debated and reused outside a single project.

Digital Frontiers

The Axioms of Literature: Core Foundations for Human and Artificial Intelligence connects literary foundations with human and artificial intelligence.

Reference & Manuals

This shelf includes The Truth That Can Be Received, The Forbidden Table, The Basement of Extremism, The Atlas of Possible Orders, The Immune State, and the freely readable The Outfinite Mind and The History and Future of Social Technologies.

Philosophical Sci-Fi

The category includes the freely readable The Gospel of the Basilisk, The Basilisk's Internal Critique of Outfinitism, and the two freely readable volumes of the Concordia series: The Noema Cycle and The Aperture Cycle.

Legitimacy Series

Holding the Dirty Thing by the Clean Side and Memes for 2030 are available as PDFs.

Social Technologies and Metacults

Metacult, Outfinitism, Decentralised Brands and Anti-Idiocracy are freely readable cultural artefacts about collective meaning, public judgment and institutional drift.

Selected published editions link to Amazon. Where a PDF is available, the presentation opens it in the browser and offers a download fallback only when the browser cannot display the PDF.
