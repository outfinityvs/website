Legal

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Outfinity collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data submitted through the website and contact form.

Data controller

The entity responsible for your personal data under this policy is the Outfinity Venture Studio operating company, as identified in the Imprint. All data processing follows applicable data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679) where it applies.

What data we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, organization name, role or title, and the content of your message. We may also collect technical information such as browser type, referring pages, and anonymized usage data through analytics technologies described in our Cookies policy.

How we use your data

We use submitted data to review your request, assess fit with current or future venture opportunities, schedule briefings, manage follow-up communication, and maintain records of our conversation. If you participate in the Explorer Circle, a project collaboration, or an investment process, additional data may be collected under separate agreement.

Legal basis for processing

We process your data based on our legitimate interest in evaluating and responding to venture, partnership, and investment inquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Where explicit consent is required for specific processing, we obtain it separately. Data provided in the context of a contractual relationship is processed based on the performance of that contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Data retention

We retain contact form data for the duration of the active conversation plus 24 months after the last communication, unless a longer retention period is required by law or justified by an ongoing relationship. You may request earlier deletion at any time through the contact form.

Data sharing and third parties

Contact form submissions are processed through Google Forms, a service provided by Google LLC. Google acts as a data processor under our instructions. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with collaborators, AI Labs, or venture partners only when necessary for the specific opportunity you have contacted us about and only with your awareness.

International data transfers

If you are located in the European Economic Area, your data may be transferred to countries outside the EEA when processed through Google Forms or other third-party services. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us through the briefing form with your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our data practices evolve. Significant changes will be communicated through the website. The current version was published on the date shown at the top of this page.
