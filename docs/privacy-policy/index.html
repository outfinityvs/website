Privacy Policy

Outfinity uses contact-form and communication data to review requests, respond to relevant conversations, and manage follow-up.

Information used for contact and qualification

When someone submits the contact form, Outfinity may use the submitted name, contact details, organization, role, message, and any other information shared in the request to review fit and prepare the next conversation.

This information supports qualification, scheduling, relationship management, and communication around the relevant venture, partner, or investor topic.

Handling and communication

Access to submitted information is limited to the people involved in qualification, follow-up, and relationship management for the relevant conversation. Information may be retained with the communication records needed to keep the discussion productive and well organized.

Privacy questions connected to a contact request can be raised through the same contact path so the request reaches the appropriate person.

Further policy details

Additional privacy details such as controller identity, legal bases, retention periods, and user-rights procedures can be published here as the website's legal setup is finalized.

Until then, this page describes the practical role of communication data in how Outfinity connects with partners.
